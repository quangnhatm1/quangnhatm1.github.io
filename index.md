# ***I. Summary***

My name is **Minh Le**. I am math teacher at [*Dayton High School
(Dayton ISD)*](https://dhs.daytonisd.net/). I am also recent graduate
from *University of Houston Downtown* (Class of 2026!), majoring in
*Applied Statistics, minoring in Mathematics*. I am also a former member
of the *UHD [Scholars
Academy](https://www.uhd.edu/academics/sciences/scholars/)* and *UHD
[Honors
Program](https://www.uhd.edu/provost/honors-program/index.aspx)*. I am
proud of being a part of Bronco family this year! I am so excited to
meet all of my sophomore, juniors, and seniors in my classroom!

# ***II. My passion***

I love math because it is like solving a puzzle. Every problem has
clues, and finding the answer feels like cracking a code. I became a
high school math teacher because I want students to feel that same
excitement, let students feel math is not a boring subject, it can be
applied everywhere in daily life. Math is not just numbers on a page, it
helps us understand money, sports, science, and even art. My goal is to
show every student that math is all around them, and that anyone can
learn it, one step at a time.

# ***III. My expectations***

In our classroom, I expect students to try their best, even when a
problem feels hard! Mistakes are okay. Why? Because they help us learn.
I ask students to listen carefully, ask questions when they are
confused, and be kind to their classmates. I expect the same from
myself: to explain things clearly, be patient, and make sure every
student feels safe asking for help!

# ***IV. My favorite math topics***

My favorite topics are **Algebra** and **Statistics**.

-   **Algebra** is something like a puzzle where letters stand in for
    missing numbers. I especially enjoy teaching systems of equations,
    because there are two clever ways to solve them, substitution and
    elimination like choosing two different paths to the same treasure.
    This is just an example why I like Algebra, there are thousands of
    reasons I love doing Algebra. Remember, if you are doing well in
    Algebra, you will beat Calculus very fast!

-   **Statistics** is about looking at real information from the data
    around our life, sports scores, student data, or survey results, and
    finding patterns hiding inside the numbers. Statistics is a branch
    of applied mathematics!

-   I also enjoy **Geometry**, because it connects math to shapes and
    the world we see every day.

# ***V. My goals for this school year***

This year, my goals are:

1.  Help every student build confidence in math, so mistakes feel like a
    normal part of learning instead of something to be afraid of.

2.  Connect math to real life, so students see why it actually matters
    outside the classroom.

3.  Make sure every student - no matter how they learn best - gets the
    support they need to succeed.

4.  Build a classroom where students feel comfortable asking questions
    and helping one another.
